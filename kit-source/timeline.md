# Timeline of the great tribulation (Chip's reading; Scripture gives the order)

Scripture states only three day counts for the end: 1,260, 1,290 and 1,335 (Daniel 12:7, 11-12; Revelation 11:3; 12:6). Everything else is placed by Revelation's own order, in general terms ("with the fourth seal," "after the fifth trumpet," "during the sixth seal"). Give no other day numbers and no future calendar dates.

## Which trumpets fall in which seal (Scripture's own wording)
- Fourth seal: trumpets 1-4, then the fifth trumpet at its close. The fourth seal strikes "the fourth part of the earth" (6:8); the first four trumpets strike "the third part" (8:7-12); the fifth trumpet opens the bottomless pit (9:1-11).
- Fifth seal: opens with the sixth trumpet. The sixth trumpet's mass death (9:13-21) and the slaying of the witnesses "when they shall have finished their testimony" (11:7) match the fifth seal's martyrs, "slain for the word of God, and for the testimony which they held" (6:9-11).
- Sixth seal: the seventh trumpet's days, the seven bowls, and the last trump at Christ's appearing. The sixth seal says "the great day of his wrath is come" (6:17); the seventh trumpet says "thy wrath is come, and the time of the dead" (11:18); the sixth seal, the seventh trumpet and the seventh bowl share the great earthquake and the fleeing islands (6:12-14; 11:19; 16:18-20). They describe one event.
- Never place all seven trumpets inside the fourth seal.

## Milestones, in order

Already fulfilled
1. The Lamb receives the book. "And he came and took the book out of the right hand of him that sat upon the throne" (Revelation 5:7); Daniel saw the same court: "there was given him dominion, and glory, and a kingdom" (Daniel 7:13-14). This research places it after the 1,260 years of the woman in the wilderness ended (1798; Revelation 12:6, 14); Scripture gives no year.
2. Seals one through three open (6:1-6). The judgment of the dead begins in heaven (1844; Daniel 8:14; 7:9-10).

Before the tribulation (future)
3. The pause. The four winds are held "till we have sealed the servants of our God in their foreheads" (7:1-3); the 144,000 are sealed (7:4).

Fourth seal (trumpets 1-5)
4. The censer is cast down: "and there were voices, and thunderings, and lightnings, and an earthquake" (8:5). The fourth seal opens (6:7-8), the winds are released, the great tribulation begins (Daniel 12:1; Matthew 24:21), and the 1,335-day count starts.
5. Trumpets one through four strike a third of the earth, the sea, the waters and the heavens (8:7-12).
6. The ten kings form a one-world government in response to the world's destruction (this research's reading). The sea beast rises with "ten horns, and upon his horns ten crowns" (13:1): Babylon, the church-state, the woman riding the scarlet beast (17:3, 7). "The dragon gave him his power, and his seat, and great authority" (13:2). It makes war with the saints "forty and two months" (13:5, 7).
7. The false prophet rises "out of the earth" with "two horns like a lamb" and speaks "as a dragon" (13:11). He "exerciseth all the power of the first beast before him," honoring and upholding it, and "doeth great wonders, so that he maketh fire come down from heaven" (13:12-13). He is an unidentified being whom Satan possesses; Satan works inside him unseen (Luke 22:3; 2 Thessalonians 2:9).
8. The fifth trumpet: Satan is released from the bottomless pit. "I saw a star fall from heaven unto the earth: and to him was given the key of the bottomless pit... And they had a king over them, which is the angel of the bottomless pit" (9:1, 11). Five months of torment fall on those "which have not the seal of God in their foreheads" (9:4-5). From here Satan works on earth in person, yet unseen, through the false prophet he possesses; he is the one who "shall ascend out of the bottomless pit" (17:8).

Fifth seal (the sixth trumpet)
9. The sixth trumpet: the four angels at the Euphrates are loosed and a third of men are slain (9:13-21); men still "repented not."
10. Satan, through the false prophet, exalts himself as God and sets up his theocracy over Babylon: "sitteth in the temple of God, shewing himself that he is God" (2 Thessalonians 2:4), "upon the glorious holy mountain" (Daniel 11:45). The false prophet makes "an image to the beast" and enforces the mark: "that no man might buy or sell, save he that had the mark" (13:14-17). The three angels' messages warn the world before mercy closes (14:6-11).
11. Martyrdom: the souls under the altar, "slain for the word of God" (6:9-11). The witnesses finish their testimony and are killed by "the beast that ascendeth out of the bottomless pit" (11:7).

The end of the 1,260 days
12. The two witnesses finish their 1,260 days (11:3), lie dead three days and a half, and rise in the sight of their enemies (11:9-12): a public sign, not the gathering.
13. Mercy closes: "He that is unjust, let him be unjust still" (22:11); "no man was able to enter into the temple, till the seven plagues of the seven angels were fulfilled" (15:8). The restrainer steps aside (2 Thessalonians 2:7). The seventh angel's days begin: "time no longer: But in the days of the voice of the seventh angel... the mystery of God should be finished" (10:6-7; 11:15).

Sixth seal (the seventh trumpet's days and the bowls)
14. Bowls one through five fall "upon the men which had the mark of the beast, and upon them which worshipped his image" (16:2-11). God's people are kept, as in Goshen (Exodus 8:22-23; Psalm 91:7-10).
15. The death decree (day 1,290): the abomination that maketh desolate (Daniel 12:11): "as many as would not worship the image of the beast should be killed" (Revelation 13:15). Esther pattern.
    Michael stands up. Scripture places it "at that time" of Daniel 11:44-45: the king of the north goes forth "with great fury to destroy, and utterly to make away many," sits on "the glorious holy mountain," "yet he shall come to his end." "And at that time shall Michael stand up... and at that time thy people shall be delivered, every one that shall be found written in the book" (Daniel 12:1). So it comes with the death decree and leads to the deliverance at His appearing; Scripture gives no separate day for it.
16. The sixth bowl: the Euphrates dried up "that the way of the kings of the east might be prepared"; three unclean spirits out of the dragon, the beast and the false prophet go "unto the kings of the earth and of the whole world, to gather them to the battle of that great day of God Almighty," and Jesus says, "Behold, I come as a thief" (16:12-16).
17. The ten kings' "one hour." When the kings see who is behind the false prophet (this research's reading; Scripture: "God hath put in their hearts to fulfil his will," 17:17), they "hate the whore... and burn her with fire" (17:16) and "give their power and strength unto the beast" (17:13), receiving "power as kings one hour with the beast" (17:12). Babylon falls in that hour (18:10, 17, 19).
18. The seventh bowl: "It is done"; the greatest earthquake; "great Babylon came in remembrance before God"; great hail (16:17-21).
19. The last trump and Christ's appearing (day 1,335: "Blessed is he that waiteth," Daniel 12:12): the sixth-seal signs, "the heaven departed as a scroll" and men hide "from the wrath of the Lamb" (6:12-17; Matthew 24:29-30); "the kingdoms of this world are become the kingdoms of our Lord" (11:15).
20. The harvest, then the vintage, the same day. First the harvest: "Thrust in thy sickle, and reap... for the harvest of the earth is ripe" (14:14-16): the dead in Christ rise and the living are caught up (1 Thessalonians 4:16-17; Matthew 24:31). Then the vintage: "gather the clusters of the vine of the earth; for her grapes are fully ripe" (14:17-20). The ten kings make war with the Lamb "and the Lamb shall overcome them" (17:14); "the remnant were slain with the sword of him that sat upon the horse" (19:19-21; Luke 17:29-30). The beast and the false prophet are cast alive into the lake of fire (19:20); Satan is bound (20:1-3).

The thousand years and after
21. The thousand years: the saints reign and judge with Christ in heaven; the earth lies empty; Satan bound (20:1-6; Jeremiah 4:23-27).
22. After the thousand years: the holy city comes down with Christ and the saints (21:2; Zechariah 14:4-5; Jude 14-15); the rest of the dead raised; Gog and Magog; the seventh seal and the Book of Life (8:1; 20:12); fire from heaven, the lake of fire, the second death (20:9-15); the new heaven and new earth (21:1).

Placing items 6-8, 10 and 17 within their seal is this research's reading of Revelation's order; Scripture states their content. No future calendar dates are published.
