# Settled positions (approved by Chip). Every entry must agree with these and with the papers.

## Style
- KJV quoted in full; Scripture explains Scripture; Scripture over commentary. Capitalize He/His/Him for God and Christ.
- Plain language for first-time readers. Label anything beyond Scripture's direct words under MY READING as Chip's reading.
- No names of people other than Chip; credit Larry Wilson only in a References line. Never say the Holy Spirit "cannot."
- Show how Chip's reading fulfills Scripture; don't spend effort refuting other views (answer common objections briefly).

## Chronology
- Daniel's seventy weeks run unbroken, 457 BC to AD 34; cross AD 30 "in the midst of the week"; Daniel 9:27's "he" is the Messiah. No seven-year tribulation; no gap.
- Daniel 12's 1,260 / 1,290 / 1,335: one continuous count from one start day, literal days (the Jubilee count ended at the seventieth Jubilee, 1994).
- We are in the "last days" (since Pentecost, Acts 2:16-17; Hebrews 1:2) but NOT at Daniel's "time of the end" nor the fourth seal. Publish no future dates.
- See timeline.md for day markers. Rev 10:6 "time no longer" belongs to the seventh angel's days (~1,260), never to the censer.
- Seals are Christ's larger campaigns; trumpets and bowls fall within them. Seals 1-3 already opened. The four horsemen are the Holy Spirit's campaign (Zechariah 6; Zechariah 4:6). Say "fourth seal," not "the four horsemen," for harm. The seventh seal (8:1) is opened after the thousand years. Revelation recaps (chapter 12 steps back to Christ's birth).

## Abomination (two uses, never merged)
- First, AD 70: Matthew 24:15, Mark 13:14, Luke 21:20, Daniel 9:26-27, 11:31. Matthew (a Jew) "stand in the holy place" = Gentile armies on holy ground around Jerusalem; Luke explains "compassed with armies."
- Second, future: Daniel 12:11, the death decree at day 1,290 (Revelation 13:15); Esther pattern.
- Never call Matthew 24:15 future from our day; never use Antiochus as a fulfillment.
- Matthew 24:15-20 = AD 70; from 24:21 = the final great tribulation (Daniel 12:1; Revelation 7:14 same "great tribulation"); 24:29-31 sixth-seal signs and gathering; Luke 21:24 shows the span. Matthew 23:36 "this generation" = AD 30-70; Matthew 24:34 = the generation that sees the sixth-seal signs.

## Gathering
- One gathering, after the tribulation, at the last trump (seventh trumpet). No pre- or mid-trib rapture; no favoritism (Romans 2:11). Believers on earth during the bowls, kept like Goshen; wrath falls on those with the mark (16:2). Persecution is the dragon's wrath, not God's. Revelation 16:15: still coming at the sixth bowl.
- At the gathering He brings the sleeping saints as He raises them (1 Thessalonians 4:14-17) and takes them where He is (John 14:3). The wicked are slain the same day.
- 1 Thessalonians 3:13 ("at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ with all his saints") is the same coming (parousia) as 4:15-17: at the last trump He brings the dead in Christ with Him (4:14), and the living are "caught up together with them... and so shall we ever be with the Lord" (4:17): all His saints together with Him. Paul uses "coming" (parousia) for this one moment four times (2:19; 3:13; 4:15; 5:23). "With all his saints" borrows Zechariah 14:5's wording; that picture is fulfilled again when He comes down with the saints and the city after the thousand years. Jude 14-15 and Zechariah 14:4-5 = His coming WITH the saints and the holy city AFTER the thousand years.
- Luke 21:36 "escape" = escape the snare/wrath, not removal. Revelation 4:1 = John's vision. Restrainer = most likely the Holy Spirit, stepping aside (not leaving) when mercy closes (~1,260); people still come to Christ "out of great tribulation" until then.
- Rapture word: "caught up" (Greek harpazo; Latin rapiemur) in 1 Thessalonians 4:17 — the event is real; its timing is the question.

## Players (never an unlabeled "the beast" in prose)
- Dragon = Satan/Lucifer, THE Antichrist (12:9); the angel of the bottomless pit (9:11; 11:7; 17:8).
- Sea beast (13:1; 17:3, 8, 11) = a kingdom and its king (Daniel 7:17, 23): the ten kings' one-world government formed out of the tribulation woes, Lucifer its king (17:13, 17).
- Babylon the whore = separate system riding the beast; burned by the ten horns (17:3, 16) in "one hour."
- Beast from the earth = the false prophet (13:11; 19:20), an unidentified being possessed by Satan; don't guess who.
- Man of sin = Lucifer fully revealed sitting as God (2 Thessalonians 2:4) on "the glorious holy mountain" (Daniel 11:45; Isaiah 14:13-16). His end: power broken at the coming (2 Thessalonians 2:8); beast and false prophet into the lake (Revelation 19:20); Satan bound 1,000 years, then the lake (20:2-3, 10; Ezekiel 28:19).
- "Many antichrists" = historical parallels. Daniel 2's feet and toes = a future coalition, NOT out of Rome.
- The mark: Scripture's purpose (buy/sell, worship, death decree; 13:15-17; 14:9-11). Technology shows how simply a mark could serve; after the first four trumpets' calamities a physical mark is probable, not definite. No identification with any technology. 666 = "the number of a man" (13:18); don't speculate on names.

## 144,000 and two witnesses
- 144,000 = God's sealed servants from every people (Galatians 3:29; tribe list points beyond ancestry: Dan missing, Levi included), firstfruits (14:4), sealed before harm (7:3), carry the two testimonies; most martyred after finishing their testimony, rest under the altar (6:9-11), rise first at the one gathering. All believers are sealed by the Spirit (Ephesians 1:13); the 144,000 are sealed for a task. Martyrdom details = Chip's reading.
- Two witnesses = God's Word in its two testimonies (Old and New, side by side, neither superseding), lit by the Spirit (Zechariah 4:6; oil), carried by His people; fire from their mouth = the Word (Jeremiah 5:14); Moses/Elijah powers = the Law and the Prophets. 1,260 days of testimony; killed when finished; Revelation 11:11-12 = a public sign, not the gathering. Don't confuse Zechariah's two olive trees with Romans 11's one olive tree.

## Thousand years and the end
- Saints reign and judge with Christ in heaven (John 14:3; 1 Corinthians 6:2-3; Revelation 20:4); earth empty (Jeremiah 4:23-27); Satan bound with no one to deceive; "the rest of the dead lived not again until the thousand years were finished" (20:5).
- After: the city comes down with Christ and the saints; wicked raised in physical form (no third resurrection); Gog and Magog = all nations since sin entered; books opened; seventh seal and Book of Life (Jesus' judgments match the names the Father kept); fire from heaven (20:9), then the lake of fire = the second death, which permanently destroys (distinct fires; Malachi 4:1-3; Ezekiel 28:18-19); then the new earth.
- Zechariah 14:16-19 and Isaiah 65:20 = conditional promises to Israel, set aside (Jeremiah 18:9-10); John goes past them ("no more curse," 22:3).
- The dead sleep (Ecclesiastes 9:5; John 11:11-14); 2 Corinthians 5:8 = next thing the believer knows is being with the Lord at the last trump.
- No third temple needed: God's people are His temple (1 Corinthians 3:16; 1 Peter 2:5); the "holy place" of the AD 70 abomination was holy ground around Jerusalem; the man of sin's "temple of God" seat is on the holy mountain (Daniel 11:45).

## Other

- Seven churches = didactic warnings. God's judgment and mercy are one God.
- USA: not named in Scripture; history ties it to the refuge of the woman in the wilderness during the 1,260 years (538-1798); religious liberty became law (Constitution Art. VI; First Amendment, 1791). No beast connection.
- Day and hour: the Noah "yet seven days" answer.

## Red flags (must not appear)
Matthew 24:15/Mark 13:14 as future or tied to Daniel 12:11; Antiochus as fulfillment; "seven-year tribulation" except describing that view; unlabeled "the beast" in prose; beast from the earth = Lucifer; Jude 14/Zechariah 14:4-5 applied to the last-trump gathering; Babylon = Jerusalem or = the sea beast; Revelation 10:6 tied to the censer; future calendar dates; "absent from the body" without the sleep clarification; "the four horsemen" for harm; "cannot" said of the Holy Spirit.
