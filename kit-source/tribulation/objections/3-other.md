# Other common objections

### O24. "The 144,000 are Jewish only" (or "they are the saints raised in Matthew 27," or "Rachel's children").
Keywords: 144,000, Jewish only, twelve tribes, firstfruits, Matthew 27:52, Rachel's children
Answer: The 144,000 are sealed on earth, as living servants, just before the harm: "Hurt not the earth... till we have sealed the servants of our God in their foreheads" (Revelation 7:3), and the locusts may hurt "only those men which have not the seal of God in their foreheads" (9:4). People already raised and in heaven wouldn't need that seal. The tribe list itself points beyond ancestry: Dan is missing, Levi (which had no land) is included, and both Joseph and his son Manasseh are named (7:5-8). Paul says who Abraham's seed is: "if ye be Christ's, then are ye Abraham's seed, and heirs according to the promise" (Galatians 3:29). So this research reads the 144,000 as God's sealed servants from every people.
They are "the firstfruits unto God and to the Lamb" (14:4), and firstfruits come right before the harvest: the 144,000 stand with the Lamb (14:1-5), the everlasting gospel goes "to every nation" (14:6), and then "the harvest of the earth is ripe" (14:15). They are described by how they lived: "they which follow the Lamb whithersoever he goeth... in their mouth was found no guile" (14:4-5). Every believer is sealed by the Spirit (Ephesians 1:13); the 144,000 are sealed for a task. In this research, most of them carry their testimony until it is finished and are martyred, resting under the altar (6:9-11) until they rise first at the one gathering.

### O25. "God never kills or destroys; Jesus corrected the Old Testament picture of God."
Keywords: God never kills, John 10:10, thief, flood, Jesus corrected the Old Testament
Answer: In John 10:10, Jesus defines the thief in the same chapter: "He that entereth not by the door into the sheepfold, but climbeth up some other way, the same is a thief and a robber" (10:1). He is contrasting Himself with the false shepherd, not saying God never judges. Jesus taught the flood as God's judgment, using the same Greek word for "destroy": "the flood came, and destroyed them all... Even thus shall it be in the day when the Son of man is revealed" (Luke 17:27, 30).
He upheld the Old Testament instead of correcting it: "Think not that I am come to destroy the law, or the prophets: I am not come to destroy, but to fulfil" (Matthew 5:17), and "the scripture cannot be broken" (John 10:35). He spoke of judgment Himself: "fear him which is able to destroy both soul and body in hell" (Matthew 10:28). And God revealed both sides of His character together: "merciful and gracious, longsuffering... and that will by no means clear the guilty" (Exodus 34:6-7). Judgment came after long mercy: "the longsuffering of God waited in the days of Noah, while the ark was a preparing" (1 Peter 3:20).

### O26. "The two witnesses are Moses and Elijah (or Enoch and Elijah)."
Keywords: two witnesses, Moses and Elijah, Enoch, Revelation 11
Answer: Let John's own clues identify them. "These are the two olive trees, and the two candlesticks standing before the God of the earth" (Revelation 11:4). Zechariah's olive trees feed the lamp with oil, and the angel explains it: "Not by might, nor by power, but by my spirit, saith the LORD of hosts" (Zechariah 4:6). Fire comes from their mouth (Revelation 11:5), and God told Jeremiah, "I will make my words in thy mouth fire" (Jeremiah 5:14). Scripture pairs the Spirit and the Word as God's witnesses: "My spirit that is upon thee, and my words which I have put in thy mouth, shall not depart out of thy mouth" (Isaiah 59:21); "the Spirit of truth... he shall testify of me" (John 15:26). They shut heaven like Elijah and turn waters to blood like Moses (11:6): the authority of the Word and the power of the Spirit, as John the Baptist came "in the spirit and power of Elias" (Luke 1:17).
So this research reads the two witnesses as the Holy Spirit and the Word of God, acting together through the sealed 144,000 for 1,260 days, so that "in their mouth was found no guile" (Revelation 14:5). Moses and Elijah show what kind of witness they bear, not who they are.

### O27. "A third temple must be rebuilt in Jerusalem for the Antichrist to defile."
Keywords: third temple, rebuilt temple, temple of God, holy place
Answer: No rebuilt temple is required. The abomination Jesus warned of stood "in the holy place" (Matthew 24:15), which Luke explains as "Jerusalem compassed with armies" (Luke 21:20): Roman armies on the holy ground around the city in AD 70. The second abomination, at day 1,290 (Daniel 12:11), is a death decree against those who refuse the image of the beast (Revelation 13:15), and it needs no building. The man of sin "as God sitteth in the temple of God" (2 Thessalonians 2:4), which Daniel describes as the holy mountain: "he shall plant the tabernacles of his palace between the seas in the glorious holy mountain" (Daniel 11:45). God's people are His temple now: "Know ye not that ye are the temple of God" (1 Corinthians 3:16), and "there is no more offering for sin" (Hebrews 10:18).

### O28. "God has two peoples, Israel and the church, with two separate programs."
Keywords: two peoples, Israel only, dispensationalism, church age, olive tree
Answer: Scripture shows one people of God. Paul pictures one olive tree, with natural branches broken off for unbelief and Gentiles grafted in by faith: "thou, being a wild olive tree, wert graffed in among them, and with them partakest of the root" (Romans 11:17), and "God is able to graff them in again" (11:23). Jesus said, "other sheep I have, which are not of this fold: them also I must bring... and there shall be one fold, and one shepherd" (John 10:16). Paul's mystery is that the Gentiles "should be fellowheirs, and of the same body" (Ephesians 3:6), and Christ "hath made both one" (2:14). "There is neither Jew nor Greek... for ye are all one in Christ Jesus. And if ye be Christ's, then are ye Abraham's seed" (Galatians 3:28-29). One people means one gathering at His coming.
