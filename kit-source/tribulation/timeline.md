# Timeline of the great tribulation (Chip's reading; Scripture gives the order)

Before day 1: the pause. The four winds are held "till we have sealed the servants of our God in their foreheads" (Revelation 7:1-3); the 144,000 are sealed (7:4).
Day 1: the censer is cast down (Revelation 8:5) = the fourth seal opens (6:7-8) = the winds are released; the trumpets begin. The 1,335-day count starts.
About day 1,260: the two witnesses finish their testimony (11:3, 7); mercy closes ("He that is unjust, let him be unjust still," 22:11; 15:8); the restrainer steps aside (2 Thessalonians 2:7); the seventh angel's days begin ("time no longer: But in the days of the voice of the seventh angel," 10:6-7); the seven last plagues begin (15:1).
Day 1,290: the abomination that maketh desolate (Daniel 12:11) = the universal death decree against all who refuse the image of the beast (Revelation 13:15), during the bowls.
About day 1,335: "Blessed is he that waiteth" (Daniel 12:12); the last trump (1 Corinthians 15:52; Revelation 11:15); the sixth-seal signs (6:12-17; Matthew 24:29); Christ appears; the dead in Christ rise and the living are caught up (1 Thessalonians 4:16-17); the wicked are slain the same day (Luke 17:29-30; Revelation 19:21).
The thousand years: the saints reign and judge with Christ in heaven; the earth lies empty; Satan bound (Revelation 20:1-6; Jeremiah 4:23-27).
After the thousand years: the holy city comes down with Christ and the saints (21:2; Zechariah 14:4-5; Jude 14-15); the rest of the dead raised; Gog and Magog; the seventh seal and the Book of Life (8:1; 20:12); fire from heaven, the lake of fire, the second death (20:9-15); the new heaven and new earth (21:1).
Day numbers inside the 1,335 are this framework's reading; only 1,260, 1,290 and 1,335 are stated in Scripture. No future calendar dates are published.
