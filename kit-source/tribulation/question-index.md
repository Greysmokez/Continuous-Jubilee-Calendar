# Question index (the top 20, in order). Answer each from the key verses, positions.md, timeline.md and the listed papers. Entries 01-04 are full worked examples.

## The Timing and Signs
01. Can we know the day or the hour? — No date can be set in advance, but watchers know it is near once the count begins, as Noah was told "yet seven days." Key: Matthew 24:32-39; Genesis 7:4, 10; 1 Thessalonians 5:1-4; Daniel 12:12. Papers: fear-not-the-second-death, jubilee-calendar-story-of-the-end. (Full entry 01.)
02. What are the signs of the Second Coming? — "The beginning of sorrows" (not yet the end), then the great tribulation's signposts in order, then the sixth-seal sky signs and the gathering. Key: Matthew 24:4-31; 2 Thessalonians 2:3-4; Daniel 12:1, 11-12; Revelation 6:12-13. Papers: the-shepherds-gathering, jubilee-calendar-story-of-the-end. (Full entry 02.)
03. Are we living in the end times right now? — In the last days since Pentecost, but not yet at Daniel's "time of the end" or the fourth seal. Key: Acts 2:16-17; Hebrews 1:1-2; Daniel 11:35, 40; 12:4, 9; Mark 13:33-37. Papers: jubilee-calendar-story-of-the-end, four-horsemen. (Full entry 03.)
04. The generation that will not pass away — Matthew 23:36 = AD 30-70; Matthew 24:34 = the generation that sees the sixth-seal signs; Luke 21:24 shows the span. Key: Matthew 23:36-38; 24:29-34; Luke 21:24-27; Psalm 95:10. Papers: five-exoduses, in-due-time, the-shepherds-gathering. (Full entry 04.)

## The Rapture
05. Rapture vs. Second Coming — One coming: He comes FOR His saints at the last trump, and they meet Him in the air; He comes WITH them and the holy city after the thousand years. Key: 1 Thessalonians 4:14-17; 1 Corinthians 15:51-52; Matthew 24:29-31; John 14:3; Revelation 21:2; Jude 14-15; Zechariah 14:4-5. Papers: the-shepherds-gathering, fear-not-the-second-death.
06. Where is the word "Rapture" in the Bible? — "Caught up" (Greek harpazo; Latin rapiemur) in 1 Thessalonians 4:17; the event is real, the timing is the question. Key: 1 Thessalonians 4:16-17; 2 Thessalonians 2:1-3. Papers: the-shepherds-gathering, pre-tribulation.
07. Pre-Tribulation vs. Post-Tribulation — No seven-year tribulation; one gathering after the tribulation at the last trump; wrath on those with the mark, God's people kept through it like Goshen. Key: Matthew 24:29-31; 2 Thessalonians 2:1-3; 1 Corinthians 15:52; Revelation 16:2; Exodus 8:22-23; John 17:15; Romans 2:11. Papers: pre-tribulation, the-shepherds-gathering.
08. Will people be left behind, and can they still be saved? — No secret rapture leaves people behind; multitudes come to Christ during the great tribulation until mercy closes (about day 1,260). "One shall be taken" (Matthew 24:40) uses paralambano, the word of "receive you unto myself" (John 14:3), so the ones taken may well be believers, but the taking happens at His coming after the tribulation (Matthew 24:29-31), per the Pre-Tribulation paper. Key: Revelation 7:9, 14; 22:11; Genesis 7:16; Matthew 24:37-41; 2 Corinthians 6:2. Papers: pre-tribulation, the-shepherds-gathering.

## The Book of Revelation Entities & Events
09. Who is the Antichrist? — The dragon, Satan, is THE Antichrist; "many antichrists" are historical parallels; the man of sin is Lucifer, working through the false prophet he possesses, revealed sitting as God on the holy mountain; the false prophet is an unidentified being he possesses. Key: Revelation 12:9; 9:11; 13:1-17; 17:8-17; 19:20; 2 Thessalonians 2:3-9; Daniel 11:45; Isaiah 14:13-16; 1 John 2:18. Papers: pre-tribulation, brittle-coalition, the-shepherds-gathering.
10. What is the Mark of the Beast (666)? — State its purpose from Scripture (buy and sell, worship, the death decree); technology shows how easily a mark could serve, but after the first four trumpets a physical mark is probable, not definite. No one takes it unknowingly; the warning comes first (14:9). Taking it is final; until mercy closes, the undecided can still accept Jesus and be sealed, but not those who took the mark. The mark and the seal are about worship and trust, not a day of the week: worship God and He provides, as with the manna, or trust Satan's theocracy and take its rations with the mark. No name speculation. Key: Revelation 13:15-18; 14:9-11; 7:3; 20:4; Exodus 16:4; Isaiah 33:16; Matthew 6:33. Papers: brittle-coalition, the-shepherds-gathering.
11. The Four Horsemen — one horseman per seal: white (seal 1, the gospel), red (seal 2, division and persecution), black (seal 3, judgment), pale (seal 4, still future, at the censer). Christ's own campaign carried out by His Spirit; seals 1-3 opened after 1798 and are still running; see "The first four seals are the four horsemen." Key: Revelation 6:1-8; Zechariah 6:1-8; Zechariah 4:6. Papers: four-horsemen.
12. Who are the Two Witnesses? — The Holy Spirit and the Word of God (the Scriptures), acting together through the sealed 144,000; not the Old and New Testaments, not two men. Key: Revelation 11:3-12; Zechariah 4:2-6, 11-14; Isaiah 59:21; Ephesians 6:17; John 15:26-27; Joel 2:23, 28; Psalm 119:105; Jeremiah 5:14; Revelation 14:5; Deuteronomy 19:15. Papers: the-shepherds-gathering, jubilee-calendar-story-of-the-end.
13. The Battle of Armageddon — A symbolic name recalling Megiddo, the great plain where kings fell; at the sixth bowl the kings of the whole world gather; it ends at His coming by "the sword of his mouth." It is separate from Gog and Magog after the thousand years. Key: Revelation 16:12-16; 19:11-21; 20:7-9; Judges 5:19; 2 Kings 23:29. Papers: the-shepherds-gathering, fear-not-the-second-death.

## Jesus' Return & The Judgments
14. How exactly will Jesus return? — Bodily, visibly, in the clouds, seen by every eye, as lightning across the sky, crowned King of kings; the wicked are slain the same day. Key: Acts 1:9-11; Matthew 24:26-31; Revelation 1:7; 19:11-16; Luke 17:29-30; Luke 19:12, 15. Papers: the-shepherds-gathering, fear-not-the-second-death.
15. The Mount of Olives Prophecy — Fulfilled when Christ returns to the earth with His saints and the holy city after the thousand years; Zechariah 14:16-19 is conditional. Key: Zechariah 14:4-5, 10-11; Acts 1:11-12; Revelation 21:2; Jeremiah 18:8-10. Papers: fear-not-the-second-death.
16. What is the Millennial Reign? — The saints reign and judge with Christ in heaven; the earth lies empty; Satan is bound with no one to deceive; the rest of the dead are raised after. Key: Revelation 20:1-6; John 14:2-3; 1 Corinthians 6:2-3; Jeremiah 4:23-27. Papers: fear-not-the-second-death, jubilee-calendar-story-of-the-end.
17. The Great White Throne Judgment — After the thousand years: the rest of the dead raised, the books and the Book of Life opened, then fire from heaven and the lake of fire, the second death. Key: Revelation 20:7-15; 8:1; Malachi 4:1-3; Ezekiel 28:18-19. Papers: fear-not-the-second-death.

## Prophetic & Israel-Related Context
18. The Third Temple — None is required. God's people are His temple; the AD 70 "holy place" was the holy ground around Jerusalem; the man of sin sits on the holy mountain. Key: 1 Corinthians 3:16; 1 Peter 2:5; Hebrews 8:13; 10:18; Matthew 24:15; Luke 21:20; Daniel 11:45. Papers: pre-tribulation, jubilee-calendar-story-of-the-end, fear-not-the-second-death.
19. Is the United States in Bible prophecy? — Not named. History ties it to the refuge of the woman in the wilderness during the 1,260 years, when religious liberty became law (Constitution Art. VI; First Amendment, 1791) before 1798. No beast connection. Key: Revelation 12:6, 14. Papers: five-exoduses, jubilee-calendar-story-of-the-end.
20. The New Heaven and the New Earth — After the second death the first earth passes away; the holy city comes down; no more death or curse; God dwells with His people. Key: Revelation 21:1-7, 27; 22:3-5; 2 Peter 3:10-13; Isaiah 65:17. Papers: fear-not-the-second-death, jubilee-calendar-story-of-the-end.
